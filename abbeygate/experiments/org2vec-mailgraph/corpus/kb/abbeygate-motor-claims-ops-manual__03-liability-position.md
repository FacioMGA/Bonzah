title: Liability position — reserve when evidence is incomplete
source: Abbeygate Motor Claims Operations Manual (Cyprus)
---

Liability must be reserved where independent evidence is incomplete: no witness statement, no CCTV, no police attendance. Where the third party disputes the version of events and no independent evidence exists, liability is reserved pending the third-party insurer's position. Reservation does not block authorising repair on a without-prejudice basis where the vehicle is undriveable or the insured's policy provides own-damage cover.
