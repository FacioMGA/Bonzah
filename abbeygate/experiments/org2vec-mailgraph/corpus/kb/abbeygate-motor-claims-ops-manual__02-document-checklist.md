title: Standard document checklist
source: Abbeygate Motor Claims Operations Manual (Cyprus)
---

Standard outstanding-document checklist applied to every open claim: motor claim form (completed), photographs of damage, photographs of scene, repair estimate(s), vehicle registration document, third-party insurer letter or details, police attendance number / police report, witness statement(s) or confirmation that none exist, medical report if bodily injury is alleged. The checklist drives the standard documents-request email.
