title: Third-party recovery process
source: TP Claims Workflow
---

Where the third party's insurer is identified and liability is accepted against them, a recovery request is issued within 5 working days of payment of the insured's own-damage indemnity. Chasers are sent at 14 and 28 days. Non-response after the second chaser is referred to the Cyprus Insurance Association reciprocal recovery process.
