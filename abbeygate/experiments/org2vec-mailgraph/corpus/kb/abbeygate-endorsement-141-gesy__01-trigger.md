Endorsement No. 141 — GHS/GESY Claims Condition (Cyprus operative). Trigger: where the Insured was confirmed at data capture to be registered with the General Health System (GESY / GHS), this endorsement applies to any claim notified under Section A of the policy (medical expenses). The endorsement does NOT apply where the Insured was confirmed at data capture as non-GESY.
