title: Fraud indicators — refer to fraud team
source: Abbeygate Motor Claims Operations Manual (Cyprus)
---

Common fraud indicators that must be referred to the fraud team: incident date predating policy inception, late notification without credible explanation, repeated claims by the same insured in short succession, repeated involvement of the same third party, photographs with metadata that contradicts the stated incident date, and estimates significantly higher than the visible damage.
