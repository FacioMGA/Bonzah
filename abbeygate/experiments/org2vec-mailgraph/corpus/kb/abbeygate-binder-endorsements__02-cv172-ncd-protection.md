title: CV172 — No-Claim Discount protection
source: Abbeygate Cyprus Binder — operative endorsement codes
---

Endorsement CV172 (No Claim Protection) is active when the insured has accrued 5+ years of unbroken NCD. With CV172 active, one fault claim in any policy year does NOT reduce the NCD. A second fault claim within the same policy year removes protection and the NCD steps down per the standard scale. Premium impact: +EUR 45 flat fee per policy year.
