THE CLIENT IS NOT ENTITLED FOR FREE ROAD ASSISTANCE WHEN:
  1. If the system shows the contract has expired and there is no renewed contract.
  2. If the vehicle is at a garage and transfer is requested to another garage (or further transport from the first garage).
  3. When the vehicle is outside a garage.
  4. If the vehicle takes part in racing competitions/activities.
  5. If the driver is under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  6. If the vehicle is in a salt lake, river, sea, mud, sand, snow or off road.
  7. If a recovery truck cannot approach due to narrow roads, underground places, multi-level car parks or obstacles.
  8. If keys are not available.
  9. If the vehicle is a van exceeding 3500kg or longer than 5m.
  10. If modifications make service/transfer difficult (e.g. very low suspension, low spoilers, cranes, tall canopy).
  11. If a truck carries load exceeding 3500kg (service only after unloading or with full responsibility accepted).
  12. If the vehicle was immobilized when the insurance contract was made.
  13. If the vehicle is at a Police Station due to a violation and not because of a technical failure.
  14. If the driver is covered through Motor Trade.
  15. If the same technical problem occurs repeatedly due to lack of repair (entitled to two free services only).
  16. When there is no spare tyre (service may be provided with charge unless factory has none).
  17. If crane/other equipment is required, the driver undertakes the expense.
  18. If weather conditions are unsuitable (storm/flood/blizzard), service may be suspended.
  19. If recovery driver evaluates damage and decides towing is not needed.
  20. Towing is entitled only towards the driver’s living town.
  21. If central locking cannot be unlocked, the driver is helped to find a locksmith at their expense.
  22. Each insured vehicle is entitled to use 3 breakdown services per year.
  23. Recovery truck can transfer up to 2 passengers.
