Significant Importance to Your Policy

Maintenance and Checking Conditions

A well-maintained building should withstand all but the most severe weather conditions.

It is important to remember that you are responsible for maintaining your home in a good state of repair. If the buildings have not been maintained in a good state of repair, when assessing a claim, we will take into account the condition of your property and we may refuse to pay the claim or we may reduce the amount of any payment we make for the claim. In some circumstances your policy may not be valid.

We will not cover the costs of repairs or replacements, where the costs arise through you failing to regularly maintain the buildings properly.

If the property suffers from some form of structural weakness that results in storm damage being greater than expected, insurers are entitled to limit their compensation to the cost of restoring the property only to its weakened state.

We will check what the weather was like when the damage happened and may ask for pictures or ask an expert to look at the damage to decide if it was caused by a storm and confirm that a lack of maintenance did not cause or contribute to the damage.

A Storm can highlight defects rather than cause them and damage due to lack of maintenance, wear and tear or which happens gradually is not covered.

These are the conditions of the insurance that you need to meet as your part of this contract.

(a) This policy covers the costs of unexpected loss or damage. It does not cover:

  1. Maintenance costs such as refixing loose roof tiles, repointing brickwork or replacing guttering. You must make sure that your Buildings are maintained in a good state of repair.

(b) We take into account the condition of the property at the time of a storm damage is said to have occurred.

You must undertake the following checks/repairs to ensure that:

  1. External walls are sealed and any gaps in external walls and foundations are restored with foam or caulk to prevent cold air and water entering the property. You might even want to consider painting the walls with a waterproof coating.
  2. Gardens are rid of overhanging, loose or dead branches to help prevent damage in windy weather.
  3. Gutters are cleared to avoid a build-up of leaves and other debris. High winds can seriously damage guttering, so regular checks throughout the year are really important. You might also want to invest in heat tape to run around the underside of your guttering to prevent ice from forming.
  4. Internal and external pipes are insulated and all external pipes and any internal pipes that are in unheated areas (e.g. in the basement or loft), with foam or rubber tubes. These tubes should then be checked regularly as they can erode over time.
  5. Visibly check the roof for any dislodged or damaged tiles which could lead to water ingress.
