title: Referral triggers (mandatory)
source: LMA9188 — Asta obo Volante and Abbeygate Cyprus DCA Agreement
---

Mandatory referral triggers: estimate or settlement exceeding the delegated authority of EUR 25,000; any bodily injury indication; any complaint referred to a regulator; any fraud indicator; any coverage dispute; any incident with potential to attract media attention or class actions. Referral is documented in the claim file before the next handler action.
