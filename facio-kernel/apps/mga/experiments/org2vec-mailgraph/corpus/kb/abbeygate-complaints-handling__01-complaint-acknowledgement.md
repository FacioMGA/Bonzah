title: Complaint acknowledgement timing
source: Complaints handling (FCA / Cyprus Insurance Commissioner)
---

Complaints must be acknowledged within one business day of receipt. A final response is required within eight weeks. Where a final response cannot be issued within eight weeks, the complainant is advised of the reason for the delay and informed of their right to refer the complaint to the Cyprus Insurance Commissioner.
