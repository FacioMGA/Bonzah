title: FNOL Motor V2 — non-approved repairer / assessor process
source: FNOL Motor V2 client letter template
---

Where the insured presents a repair estimate from a garage that is not an Abbeygate approved or nominated repairer, the handler must instruct an approved assessor to inspect the vehicle at the insured's choice of garage. The assessor reports to the insurer. Excess applies to settlement; insured pays the excess directly to the repairer and signs a discharge receipt on completion.
