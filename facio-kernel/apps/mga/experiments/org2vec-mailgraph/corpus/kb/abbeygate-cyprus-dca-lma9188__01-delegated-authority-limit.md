title: Delegated authority — EUR 25,000 per claim
source: LMA9188 — Asta obo Volante and Abbeygate Cyprus DCA Agreement
---

The coverholder's delegated claims authority is EUR 25,000 per claim inclusive of indemnity and expenses. Any estimated incurred or any proposed settlement that exceeds EUR 25,000 must be referred to the managing agent before payment or settlement is agreed. Authority is not transferable and may not be sub-delegated.
