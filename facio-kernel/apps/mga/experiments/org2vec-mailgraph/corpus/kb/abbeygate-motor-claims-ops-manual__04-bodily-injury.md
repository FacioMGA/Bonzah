title: Bodily injury — flagging and reserving
source: Abbeygate Motor Claims Operations Manual (Cyprus)
---

If the insured or any third party reports pain, attends medical treatment, or signals an intention to pursue a bodily injury claim, the handler must flag BI potential and adjust reserves to reflect indemnity exposure. A medical report must be requested. BI claims are not within the standard own-damage authority and may require escalation regardless of monetary threshold.
