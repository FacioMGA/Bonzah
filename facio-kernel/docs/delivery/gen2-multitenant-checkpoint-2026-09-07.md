## Delivered implementation

- Organization invitations and active membership authorize workspace creation/selection. Operating Tenant, organization and policyholder Account remain distinct identifiers. Roles and persisted sessions are checked server-side; no global role grants another tenant's records.
- A real MGA creation/profile form captures legal and trading identity, role, contact, address, logo, colors, jurisdiction, currency, locale/time zone and registered portfolio. Creation provisions its own existing product/programme, authority, rating and questionnaire records transactionally and idempotently.
- PostgreSQL/pgvector stores the application, identity and configuration data. The runtime login is neither superuser nor BYPASSRLS. Forced row policies and tenant-aware transactions protect business queries and worker execution. Policy/quote/certificate numbering is collision-safe across tenants.
- Workspace-specific branding loads into the existing application and generated documents. No customer contacts, payment credentials, signed appointments or customer records are supplied by the reusable training portfolios.
- Symphony configuration is authored in the existing back office, HTTP API and authenticated MCP through shared services. Publication requires the exact draft hash and explicit authorities; published policy decisions retain their configuration version. Compiler 3 explicitly materializes required engine questions at publication. The follow-up compiler 4 implements inherited coverage/segment/section/binder question scopes and retains instruction text as read-only paragraphs; Commercial segment choices use the same registered profession/Ready-line projection as pricing. These changes require an explicit new draft and publication; old definitions are preserved. Public compiler 4 verification passed: the configured scope switches question visibility and required-question totals while retaining the unsaved answer; the 390px page has no horizontal overflow.
- Starting portfolios contain the applicable existing Home, Travel, Health and Motor engines, plus the configured Commercial engine. Unsupported territory/product combinations are rejected. External payments and delivery remain explicitly disabled until configured.
