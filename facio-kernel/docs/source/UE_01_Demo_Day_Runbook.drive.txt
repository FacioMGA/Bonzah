Primary scenario and source preparation
Use Randolph Community College as the primary institution for document intake and underwriting review. Its package contains an application, a statement of values, a loss-run workbook, and a property appraisal. Keep all source periods visible. The package is a submission example, not evidence that UE has bound it or accepted the values for the new property program. Randolph source folder.
The source guide records exact values and locators. On sheet Schedule of Value - 2025-08-28T, cell G45 totals $163,260,053, while G47 displays $161,957,303. The $1,302,750 difference equals the renovation amount in F45. The row-45 total includes the renovation amount, while the displayed grand total does not. Show both totals and their source references, explain the unresolved treatment, and route the difference for review. Never silently select the larger total as the approved exposure.
The appraisal reports $163,161,600 with a different valuation date and includes property in the open. It is not an interchangeable third SOV total. The application lists more premises than the SOV site codes, including vacant land and a leased site. These differences require scope and date reconciliation, rather than a blanket assertion that the documents are wrong.
Use the actual application to populate institution and broker information where legible. Load the original files once, preserve their names, and display the document list. A separate demo fixture should carry synthetic quote options, policy lifecycle events, invoices, payments, commissions, and claim notifications. Prefix fixture identifiers with DEMO-UE and keep them visibly separate from source policy numbers.

Live walkthrough and presenter prompts
Review and external enrichment
Show the underwriting queue and the institution’s unresolved items. Resolve one item only if the demo has an approved example resolution; otherwise retain its review state and continue on a separate prepared case. Show external data next to the submitted field with source, retrieval time and match status. If there is no actual provider connection, use a labeled prepared response or discuss the integration contract.
Presenter prompt: “Which external information must be available before an underwriter can quote, and which differences require referral rather than automatic replacement?”
Rating and quote options
Show the inputs used for rating, their approved state, the calculation or engine version, and the returned price/terms. UE’s rate manual and engine contract are customer dependencies. Synthetic prices must remain labeled. Compare two prepared quote options, select one, and show the selected version carried forward without ambiguity.
Presenter prompt: “Where should your rating engine remain authoritative, and which approved examples should we use to prove the integration?”
Binding and policy administration
Show prerequisites and subjectivities, the approval step, and the link from selected quote to policy. Open the policy timeline and its issued or prepared documents. Demonstrate a blocked bind where a required condition is unmet if the application supports it. Explain the authority check using the actual demo behavior, not an inferred control.
Presenter prompt: “Who can authorize binding, and what must be complete before a policy or binder can be issued?”
Billing and commissions
Open the policy’s invoice and receivable. Apply only a synthetic prepared receipt in the demo environment, then show the outstanding amount and commission treatment. Keep calculated commission, payable commission, and paid commission separate. If finance is currently a prepared example, state that before showing it.
Presenter prompt: “Will the property program use agency bill or direct bill, and which system will own cash allocation and the accounting ledger?”
Endorsement, FNOL, renewal and cancellation
Use separate branches of the prepared policy so the history stays coherent. Branch A adds a synthetic midterm change, shows its effective date and premium effect, and later opens a renewal comparison. Branch B records a synthetic property incident linked to the correct policy term. Branch C demonstrates cancellation and return-premium review on a separate copy. Do not show a cancelled policy proceeding through an ordinary active-policy renewal without explaining reinstatement or the alternate branch.
Show the financial consequence of the endorsement or cancellation in the same billing and commission views used earlier. For FNOL, show capture and handoff rather than a claim-coverage or settlement decision.
Reporting
Open a report based on the displayed policy and transaction records. Show the as-of date, filters, metric definitions, totals and a drill-through to the originating transaction. Where export is available, verify that values and IDs survive the export and distinguish policy premium from billed, collected, outstanding and refunded amounts.

The exposure templates contain stale Menta Group material that must not enter this demonstration. Confirm the presenter can access source documents and that the shared-screen display is readable.
Use a prepared screenshot or recording only if it comes from an actual run and is labeled with its context. A slide illustrating expected behavior should be described as a design example.
