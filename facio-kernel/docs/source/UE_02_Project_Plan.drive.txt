Requirements for intake and underwriting
Each requirement below carries forward one of UE’s explicitly requested modules. The demonstration action and acceptance condition are proposed. A rehearsal record should identify the environment, application version, case, result, evidence, and any fallback.
R01 Broker Management
Maintain the brokerage, producer, institution relationship, contacts, and responsible underwriter. Demonstrate opening an institution from its broker and showing the same relationship on its submission and policy. Acceptance: a broker user sees authorized institutions only; changing the servicing relationship preserves history. UE must supply the authority, appointment, licensing, and brokerage hierarchy rules that apply to the property program.
R02 Document Intake and Submission Intake and Ingestion
Accept the supplied PDF and spreadsheet package, retain originals, classify document types, and map institution and location data with source references. Demonstrate a missing item and a discrepancy before review completion. Acceptance: extraction produces reviewable values with document and page or sheet/cell references; repeat intake avoids duplicate business records; conflicting values remain visible. The source corpus includes scanned forms, multi-sheet workbooks, current exposure schedules, and older policy specimens.
R03 Underwriting
Present locations, insured values, construction and protection fields, loss information, missing information, and referrals as one review. Demonstrate an underwriter resolving a discrepancy with an explanation and recording a decision. Acceptance: only an authorized person can approve a referred case, and the application records the previous value, new value, reviewer, time, and reason. The authority matrix and appetite rules remain customer inputs.
R04 External Data Source Enrichment
Associate external information with the correct address or institution while retaining provider, query time, match quality, and the original submitted value. Demonstrate a matching result and a provider failure or uncertain match. Acceptance: uncertain or unavailable enrichment does not become an invented value or silently replace submission data. Data providers, permitted uses, credentials, costs, and licensing are discovery items.
R05 Rating
Send approved exposure and coverage inputs to a versioned rating service or configured calculation and keep its input/output record. The meeting recap discusses API integration for rating; the API contract and engine are unverified. Acceptance: known reference cases reproduce UE-approved premiums and factors; missing required fields or failed rating responses prevent an apparently valid quote. Any illustrative rate used in the demo must be visibly identified as a demo assumption.
Requirements for quoting and policy transactions
R06 Quote Management
Create quote options from a reviewed submission, compare terms, and retain each option’s rating version, limits, deductibles, premium, fees, and validity. Demonstrate selecting one version. Acceptance: subsequent edits create a new version or explicitly controlled change, and the bind action uses the selected approved version. Expired, withdrawn, or superseded options cannot bind through an unnoticed stale state.
R07 Binding
Check authority, required documents, subjectivities, dates, and approved terms before producing a policy record. Demonstrate the complete handoff from accepted quote to policy and the blocked path when a condition is unmet. Acceptance: one accepted quote yields one authorized binding transaction and the correct document pack. Capacity, carrier, jurisdiction, signature, numbering, and issuance rules need UE approval.
R08 Endorsements
Create an effective-dated change to a policy without overwriting its prior state. Demonstrate a synthetic location or value change and its premium effect, linked to the resulting documents and financial transaction. Acceptance: the prior policy remains reconstructable; the adjusted period, premium, receivable, and commission agree. Out-of-sequence changes and overlapping effective dates require explicit behavior.
R09 FNOL Intake
Capture the insured, loss date and location, event narrative, documents, reporter, and policy reference, then route the report to its owner. Demonstrate a clearly labeled synthetic property incident and a possible duplicate. Acceptance: the report retains its provenance, linked policy version, status, and acknowledgement record. FNOL intake does not imply claims adjudication, settlement, or an automated coverage determination.
R10 Renewals
Create a new term with the expiring policy, requested updates, current exposures, loss history, and a changes comparison. Demonstrate an updated schedule and referral before a renewal quote. Acceptance: changes and missing renewal information remain visible; old and new terms are distinct, with decisions and rating versions traceable. Customer-specific notice windows, nonrenewal rules, and referral thresholds remain open.
Requirements for finance and administration
R11 Cancellations and Refunds
Record the cancellation reason, request and effective dates, applicable rule, notice workflow, earned premium, return premium, and refund state. Demonstrate a synthetic cancellation as a separate branch from the continuing renewal case. Acceptance: approved examples reproduce the correct return premium and related tax, fee, receivable, and commission treatment; issued notices and payment actions require authorized release. Do not assume pro rata cancellation is always applicable.
R12 Commission Tracking and Broker Commissions
Apply an approved broker agreement to the correct premium base and transaction, keeping earned, payable, paid, and reversed amounts distinct. Demonstrate a new-business accrual and an endorsement or cancellation adjustment. Acceptance: a transaction does not create duplicate commission; rates and bases remain explainable; reversals reconcile to the original. Agreements, splits, timing, tax treatment, and exception authority need confirmation.
R13 Billing and Receivables
Connect policy transactions to invoices, installments, receipts, allocations, credits, refunds, and outstanding balances. Demonstrate an invoice, a partial payment, and an aged balance using synthetic amounts. Acceptance: the balance equals charges less applied receipts and credits; unmatched money remains unapplied; refund status distinguishes calculated, approved, and paid. Confirm direct versus agency bill, payment service, bank or ledger interfaces, currency, and reconciliation cadence.
R14 Policy Administration and Management
Show the institution’s policy timeline, terms, documents, participants, activities, and transaction history. Demonstrate finding a prior version and the documents issued from it. Acceptance: policy identity remains stable while transactions and effective-dated versions are explicit; user permissions govern access and change. Agree the system of record for each object and the migration or coexistence strategy.
R15 Reporting
Provide traceable views of submission progress, referrals, quote outcomes, policy transactions, receivables, and commissions. Demonstrate drilling into one aggregate to its underlying records. Acceptance: as-of date, transaction versus effective date, currency, filters, and calculation definitions are explicit; report totals reconcile to source records and exports. Regulatory, carrier, reinsurance, and management reporting requirements need named owners and approved layouts.
Requirements beyond the walkthrough
Production scope must also settle role permissions, identity management, audit retention, document access, encryption and key ownership, integrations, exports, monitoring, support, disaster recovery, and release rollback. These are delivery decisions, not assertions of existing certifications or contractual service levels. Record what Facio already supports with evidence and price any additional work only after the implementation boundary is clear.
Data and integration design
Use a shared institution identity across the broker relationship, submission, quote, policy, claim notification, and financial records. Retain location IDs within each institution. Give original documents stable IDs and versions. Associate every extracted fact with the document version, page or sheet/cell, and extraction/review state.
The proposed business record sequence is submission, underwriting review, rating run, quote version, binding transaction, policy version, and financial postings. Endorsements, renewals, cancellations, and FNOL reference the relevant term and effective date. They should not overwrite the original submission or prior policy history.

Pilot success and acceptance
Agree a baseline before stating a speed or efficiency gain. Measure handling time using equivalent cases, separating elapsed waiting time from active user work. Track extraction accuracy by field type, document type and materiality; include both correct values and correct identification of missing or conflicting data. Report the sample size and exception rate with each result.
Suggested pilot checks are exact agreement on institution identity, effective dates, selected quote version, and policy reference; reconciled monetary totals to the approved cent or documented rounding rule; traceable source references for every displayed material fact; and explicit review for every unresolved discrepancy. UE and Facio should agree numeric accuracy and turnaround thresholds from the pilot corpus, rather than adopting unsupported marketing percentages.
Exercise at least these scenarios: clean submission; missing required document; conflicting totals; duplicate upload; scanned input; provider timeout; unauthorized user; underwriting referral; stale quote; bind retry; effective-dated endorsement; renewal changes; cancellation reversal; partial receipt; unmatched receipt; commission reversal; duplicate FNOL; and report drill-through. A failure must identify the affected case and business consequence.
