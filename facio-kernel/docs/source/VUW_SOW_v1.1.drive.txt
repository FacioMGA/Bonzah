Executive summary
This SOW defines the FacioMGA implementation for Vuw.ai as the system of record, regulatory reporting, and financial operations layer sitting behind Vuw's in-house underwriting and risk engine. Vuw ingests and underwrites business through its own front end; Facio receives the bound and quoted risk data via API, records it in a Lloyd's-ready policy administration system and risk ledger, produces the required documents and bordereaux, and manages the financial layer: banking reconciliation, commissions, and capacity reporting.
The engagement begins with a Proof of Concept (three working sessions across approximately one month) at no cost. The commercial subscription follows the Microsoft Managed Success structure for 12 months and commences upon successful POC completion, targeting go-live readiness by mid-September 2026. The Facio Cloud Service Level Agreement takes effect with the paid subscription and does not apply during the free POC.
Initial programs: (1) US regional-mutual property quota-share reinsurance following Munich Re and Swiss Re; (2) Middle East cargo insurance placed into the Lloyd's market via a London broker with a multi-insurer tower; (3) property reinsurance for a Caribbean reinsurer via a major London broker. Initial capacity: two Lloyd's syndicates and one Caribbean reinsurer.

01  ·  BACKGROUND
Background.
Vuw.ai is building an MGA operation that ingests business from multiple brokers, regions, and channels (Whitespace in the Lloyd's market, email, and Excel bordereaux) into its proprietary front end and underwriting engine. Vuw's engine handles submission intake, risk assessment, and underwriting decisioning, including auto-bind for risks within the capacity providers' appetite and referral workflows where required.
Vuw requires everything downstream of the underwriting decision: a clean system of record, a risk ledger tightly connected to an accounting ledger, document production, Lloyd's-standard reporting, and a financial layer covering bordereaux production, banking reconciliation, and commission management. Underwriters operate only in the underwriting engine and the policy administration system, never in email.
Vuw does not yet hold Lloyd's coverholder status and operates as an Appointed Representative through ProGlobal. In the first iteration Vuw will trade as ProGlobal, which will act as coverholder. This is a legal-structure consideration only and does not change the scope of this SOW; the platform configuration will reflect the ProGlobal coverholder arrangement.
Initial programs
* Program 1, US property reinsurance (regional mutual). Localized office properties in the US, non-cat risks initially, on a quota-share basis behind a 50% line, following Munich Re and Swiss Re. Early cases are expected to route through referral workflows while appetite-rule interpretation is calibrated.
* Program 2, cargo insurance (Middle East book). Direct insurance business entering London via a London broker, placed in the Lloyd's market with a tower of multiple insurers behind the lead capacity provider. The capacity provider's in-house placing broker participates in the flow. This program will be subject to Lloyd's audit.
* Program 3, property reinsurance (Caribbean reinsurer). Reinsurance product placed through a major London broker into London.
Initial capacity: two Lloyd's syndicates and one Caribbean reinsurer. Brokers are expected to be predominantly, but not necessarily exclusively, Lloyd's brokers. Additional programs are under development and can be added under the same architecture.
Objective
Implement FacioMGA as Vuw's Azure-hosted system of record and financial operations platform: receive underwritten risk data from Vuw's engine via API, administer the full policy lifecycle, produce broker statements and regulatory outputs, generate Lloyd's-standard bordereaux, and reconcile banking transactions against the risk and accounting ledgers.
02  ·  GOALS & OUTCOMES
Goals & business outcomes.
* Clean system of record. Every quote and bound risk from Vuw's engine is recorded in the PAS, satisfying Lloyd's requirements for quote recording and full lifecycle auditability.
* Lloyd's-first reporting. Premium, risk, and claims bordereaux in Lloyd's standard out of the box, mapped to Lloyd's CR codes for the agreed classes of business.
* Financial control. Risk ledger tightly connected to the accounting ledger, with intelligent bank reconciliation linking funds movements to risk transactions, exposure, and claims.
* Unified claims visibility. Monthly intake of claims bordereaux from multiple insurers (all claims handled in-house by insurers; no TPAs), consolidated into one unified claims bordereau.
* Audit readiness. Auditor interface with granted access so Lloyd's auditors work directly in the system, reducing manual audit overhead for the audited programs (e.g., cargo).
* Multi-capacity complexity. Support for towers with multiple insurers on one risk, quota-share follow structures, and reporting across two Lloyd's syndicates plus a non-Lloyd's Caribbean reinsurer.
Success metrics, POC
* Agreed data schema, mapped to Lloyd's CR codes for Vuw's classes of business, accepted end-to-end via API.
* Policies issued correctly from Vuw engine output, including midterm amendments and endorsements.
* Premium bordereau generated in Lloyd's standard from recorded risks.
* Reconciliation demonstrated on synthesized bank transactions against the known Revolut schema.
* Broker statement produced electronically from a Vuw-branded template.
03  ·  SCOPE OF WORK
Scope of work.
3.1 System of record & policy administration
* Recording of every quote produced by Vuw's engine in the PAS, per Lloyd's requirements.
* Full policy lifecycle: bind, issue, midterm amendments, endorsements, cancellation, and renewal, with version history and audit logging.
* Configuration for the ProGlobal coverholder legal structure and the reinsurance/insurance program distinctions (cedent-facing reinsurance vs. direct cargo insurance).
* Multi-capacity support: towers with multiple insurers on one risk, quota-share follow behind a 50% line, and per-capacity allocation across two Lloyd's syndicates and one Caribbean reinsurer.
* Optional risk-level granularity: rating breakdown beyond the approved wording or schedule (e.g., peril-level splits for property) recorded into the risk ledger to enrich downstream risk reporting; pass-through of final premium alone is also supported.
3.2 Integration & data schema
* REST API integration from Vuw's underwriting engine into the PAS; MCP server available for AI-agent-driven interaction; full GUI access included. API, UI, and MCP may be used interchangeably.
* Facio-generated data schema based on Vuw's risk codes (classes of business), pre-mapped to Lloyd's CR codes, with mandatory and recommended fields marked.
* Joint mapping session to align Vuw's schema (object-oriented or otherwise) to the minimum required dataset; North America assumed as the initial risk-location specification, with other regions added as needed.
* Support for dynamic or constant schema approaches as preferred by Vuw's engineering team.
3.3 Document generation
* Broker statements issued electronically from a Vuw-supplied Word template (logo and content as provided), the only client-issued document required by the current business model.
* All regulatory-required artifacts and outputs produced by Facio as part of the platform's Lloyd's-ready reporting layer; no policy-document issuance obligation exists for Vuw in the initial programs.
3.4 Bordereaux, Lloyd's reporting & audit
* Premium bordereau and risk bordereau in Lloyd's standard, mapped via Facio's CR-code libraries for the agreed classes of business.
* Monthly intake of claims bordereaux from the insurers (3 to 20+ files per month), consolidated into a unified claims bordereau; claims feed recorded for Vuw's visibility. All claims are handled in-house by the insurers, so no TPA integration is required.
* Audit center with auditor interface: access-granted Lloyd's auditors work directly in the system for the audited programs.
* Reporting configuration across the Lloyd's syndicates and the Caribbean reinsurer, demonstrating the mixed-capacity reporting complexity.
3.5 Financial layer: reconciliation, banking & commissions
* Risk ledger tightly connected to the accounting ledger as the core financial system of record.
* Intelligent reconciliation engine linking bank funds movements to risk transactions, exposure, and claims, with an easy-to-use interface for manual linking of exceptions.
* Banking connectivity via UK Open Banking: Revolut assumed first (known schema, no client action needed); HSBC supported subject to a CSV transaction export sample to validate the schema.
* Commission calculation and capacity/broker splits recorded against the risk ledger.
* Financial reports required for filing, generated from the reconciled ledgers.
* Reconciliation available through the API as well, allowing Vuw to build its own screens inside its front end if preferred.
04  ·  PROOF OF CONCEPT
Proof of concept plan.
The POC compresses the full core handshake set into three working sessions across approximately one month, at no cost to Vuw. The session of Thursday, August 13 is the first of the three and forms part of the POC process.
* Session 1, schema & scope (Thursday, August 13, 2026). Review of the Lloyd's CR-mapped schema generated from Vuw's risk codes; joint mapping of Vuw's schema to the minimum required dataset; broker statement template and bank confirmation locked.
* Session 2, issuance & reporting (week of August 17, 2026). Demonstrate correct policy issuance from Vuw engine data, including midterm amendments and endorsements, the area where policy admin systems typically become messy; premium bordereau generated in Lloyd's standard; audit center walk-through configured for the cargo program.
* Session 3, financial simulation & completion (week of August 31, 2026). Reconciliation demonstrated on synthesized bank transactions (no live bank integration during the POC); integration handshake test validated; confidential demonstration of the reconciliation engine operating at scale in existing production deployments; POC completion review.
The purpose of the POC is to reach sufficient confidence to continue at full pace toward the September demonstration to Vuw's mandates: realistic, demonstrable, and without shortcuts. POC success is defined in Section 11, Acceptance Criteria. The Facio Cloud Service Level Agreement does not apply during the free POC; it takes effect upon commencement of the paid subscription.

* Auditability: immutable workflow and event history for data changes, document issuance, user actions, and financial movements.
