30-Day POC Milestones Plan.
Working document for joint review, comments and approval. Comment directly in this document; each gate has a dedicated space for VUW.ai feedback.


Objective
	Prove that Facio can operate as VUW.ai's authoritative system of record, policy administration, reporting and reconciliation layer, providing sufficient confidence to proceed into production.
	POC model
	Five Friday gates across the Microsoft Marketplace 30-day free trial. The trial starts only when the environment and minimum inputs are ready.
	Primary focus
	US property reinsurance, using representative risks and capacity structure supplied by VUW.ai.
	

00 · READINESS GATE
Readiness before Day 1.
* Microsoft Private Offer accepted and the VUW.ai environment available.
* Representative risks, available submission packs and capacity structure received.
* Facio has configured the initial product, schema, endpoint and staging environment.
* Both teams have confirmed their technical and business acceptance owners.


The 30-day trial clock begins only after this readiness gate is met.
	

01 · FRIDAY · WEEK 1
Meeting 1: Configured API and schema review.
* Review the configured VUW.ai API endpoint and property-reinsurance schema.
* Review documented authentication, payloads, responses and lifecycle events.
* Validate quote, bind, referral, rejection and endorsement event handling.
* Review evidence storage, Lloyd's risk-code mapping and relevant CDR mapping.
* Review VUW.ai-specific API documentation.


OUTCOME
	VUW.ai confirms that the configured endpoint, schema, authentication and lifecycle-event model provide a viable integration contract.
	

02 · FRIDAY · WEEK 2
Meeting 2: Submission, quote and underwriting workflow.
* Submit a representative risk to Facio through the API.
* Retain the original submission pack and extracted data as linked evidence.
* Demonstrate Simplified ID sanctions screening, quote creation and versioning.
* Demonstrate referral and underwriting collaboration inside Facio.
* Return relevant status and outcome events to VUW.ai.


OUTCOME
	The submission-to-quote workflow succeeds without manual rekeying and retains a complete audit trail.
	

03 · FRIDAY · WEEK 3
Meeting 3: Bind, capacity and policy lifecycle.
* Demonstrate bind confirmation and lead/follow or quota-share allocation.
* Generate the agreed broker statement.
* Process one endorsement or midterm amendment.
* Verify policy version history, evidence and financial transaction impact.
* Validate access through both the Facio interface and API.


OUTCOME
	Facio successfully administers the representative risk through bind and a post-bind policy change.
	

04 · FRIDAY · WEEK 4
Meeting 4: Reporting, reconciliation and end-to-end test.
* Generate the premium bordereau from the POC transactions.
* Demonstrate reconciliation using representative synthetic bank transactions.
* Resolve at least one reconciliation exception manually.
* Demonstrate auditor and management visibility.
* Run a repeatable end-to-end test, including staging reset.


OUTCOME
	The policy, reporting, audit and financial handshakes operate successfully as one connected process.
	

05 · FRIDAY · FINAL REVIEW
Meeting 5: Final POC review and continuation decision.
* Review all agreed POC success criteria.
* Classify remaining items as blockers or post-POC enhancements.
* Confirm production priorities and the initial roadmap for cargo and Caribbean programmes.
* Decide whether to continue into the paid Microsoft subscription and production project.


OUTCOME
	Joint decision recorded, with an agreed production path or a clearly documented reason not to proceed.
	

06 · SUCCESS CRITERIA
Final POC success criteria.
* The configured API and schema integrate credibly with VUW.ai's underwriting engine.
* Facio can serve as the authoritative system of record.
* Submission evidence and lifecycle events are retained and auditable.
* Quote, bind, capacity allocation and endorsement workflows operate correctly.
* The agreed document, bordereau and reconciliation outputs are demonstrated.
* The implementation provides a reusable foundation for production and additional programmes.


07 · CADENCE
Working cadence.
* Formal gate review: Friday at 12:00 noon UK time.
* Facio implementation continues between weekly reviews.
* Technical working sessions may be scheduled between gates where required.
* Priorities may be adjusted jointly without changing the agreed final success test.


08 · APPROVAL
Joint approval.
