Official website context
UE’s public business is education-specific liability insurance and risk management for K–12 schools, colleges and universities. It was founded in 1987 by 59 educational institutions and reports an AM Best A (Excellent) rating each year since 1998. These facts explain the education and risk-management context; they do not establish the operating rules of the new property MGA discussed with Facio. About UE.
Public products include Educators Legal Liability (ELL), General Liability (GL), Internships and Professional Services (IPL), and Public School Liability/School Board Legal Liability (PSL/SBL). Primary, excess and buffer products have different terms. Current ELL and GL pages state capacity up to $35 million subject to underwriting; an older overview PDF showed $30 million. Use the current specific page with its date and qualifications, and do not transplant liability limits into the property program. ELL; GL; IPL; Public K–12.
For new business, UE instructs brokers to provide a completed UE application, six-year carrier loss runs and the institution’s most recent audited financials to the territory manager. Application selection varies by institution type, enrollment and requested products, with exposure-specific supplements. These are documented public liability-submission practices, not proof that every supplied property package is complete or governed by the same requirements. For Brokers; Business Applications.
UE already publicly documents My UE for Members and My UE for Brokers, covering renewal status, claims lists, institution/brokerage contacts and account permissions. Some detailed guides require authentication and were not read. The website therefore supports asking how existing portals and systems fit the project; it does not support saying UE lacks a portal or already integrates with Facio. Knowledge Center.
The claim page distinguishes Claim/FNOL, Notification Only and ProResponse service requests. Previously reported matters go to the assigned Resolutions analyst. These distinctions are useful terminology but do not define the proposed property claims integration. Report a Claim.
Risk-management resources address education exposures such as athletics, youth protection, workforce, campus violence, facilities, travel and technology. RMPC is an eligibility-based renewal premium-credit program with activity and evidence deadlines, not a universal discount or cash rebate. UE’s 2026 public large-loss report concerns publicly reported education settlements/awards, not UE’s own insured loss dataset. Neither public risk rankings nor AI-risk guidance establishes UE’s internal rating rules or IT requirements. Risk Management; RMPC; Large Loss Report.

The source corpus should remain within its authorized evaluation context. A factual absence in the supplied files should be recorded as missing, not filled with a guessed customer fact.
